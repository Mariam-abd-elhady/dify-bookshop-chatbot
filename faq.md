# Korashi Books — Frequently Asked Questions

Korashi Books is an independent bookshop in Cairo selling Arabic and English titles, with a small café corner and a special-orders desk for hard-to-find books.

## 1. What are your opening hours?
We're open Saturday to Thursday, 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. On Fridays we open later, at 2:00 PM, and close at 10:00 PM.

## 2. Where are you located?
Our shop is in Downtown Cairo, on Talaat Harb Street, a two-minute walk from Talaat Harb metro station. Look for the green awning with the open-book logo.

## 3. Do you ship outside Cairo?
Yes. We ship nationwide within Egypt through Bosta. Cairo and Giza orders usually arrive in 1–2 business days; other governorates (including Alexandria) take 3–5 business days. Shipping fees are calculated at checkout based on your address and order weight.

## 4. What is your return policy?
You can return any unused, unmarked book within 14 days of purchase with your receipt for a full refund or exchange. Sale items and special orders are final sale and cannot be returned.

## 5. What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, Visa/Mastercard (credit and debit), and Instapay for both in-store and online orders. We do not currently accept installment plans.

## 6. Do you have books in English?
Yes. About a third of our stock is in English, covering fiction, business, self-help, and children's books. If you're looking for a specific English title, message us and we'll check availability before you visit.

## 7. Can you special-order a book you don't have in stock?
Yes. We can special-order most books available through our distributors, both Arabic and English titles. Special orders typically take 5–10 business days to arrive, and we ask for a 50% deposit upfront.

## 8. How can I contact you?
You can reach us on Instagram or WhatsApp at @KorashiBooks, or by phone at the shop between 10:00 AM and 10:00 PM. We usually reply within a few hours during opening times.

## 9. Do you host any events?
Yes, we hold a book club meetup on the first Thursday of every month at 7:00 PM, and occasional author signings — announced on our Instagram page.

## 10. Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes, gift wrapping is free for any purchase and available both in-store and for delivery orders — just add a note at checkout or ask a staff member.
